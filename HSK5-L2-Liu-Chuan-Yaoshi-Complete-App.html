段落 ① · 第 1 句
父母一辈子住在农村老家，对老屋的感情，就像没断奶的孩子对母亲一样。
Fùmǔ yí bèizi zhù zài nóngcūn lǎojiā, duì lǎowū de gǎnqíng, jiù xiàng méi duàn nǎi de háizi duì mǔqīn yíyàng.
My parents have lived their whole lives in the old family home in the countryside; their feeling for that old house is like an unweaned child’s for its mother.
段落 ① · 第 2 句
因此长年以来，父母很少离开老屋，尽管姥姥、舅舅和姑姑都在城里，父母也坚决不在城里住。
Yīncǐ chángnián yǐlái, fùmǔ hěn shǎo líkāi lǎowū, jǐnguǎn lǎolao, jiùjiu hé gūgu dōu zài chénglǐ, fùmǔ yě jiānjué bú zài chénglǐ zhù.
So for many years they rarely left it. Even though Grandma, Uncle and Auntie all lived in town, my parents flatly refused to live there.
段落 ② · 第 3 句
去年，在我和妻子的努力下，我们终于用打工挣的钱，在县里买了一套新房。
Qùnián, zài wǒ hé qīzi de nǔlì xià, wǒmen zhōngyú yòng dǎgōng zhèng de qián, zài xiàn lǐ mǎi le yí tào xīnfáng.
Last year, with my wife’s effort and mine, we finally bought a new flat in the county town with money we had earned working away from home.
段落 ② · 第 4 句
新房装修完，父母第一次走进新房时，高兴得不得了。
Xīnfáng zhuāngxiū wán, fùmǔ dì-yī cì zǒu jìn xīnfáng shí, gāoxìng de bùdéliǎo.
When the flat had been fitted out and my parents walked into it for the first time, they were overjoyed.
段落 ② · 第 5 句
妻子提出留一串钥匙给父母，可他们拒绝了。
Qīzi tíchū liú yí chuàn yàoshi gěi fùmǔ, kě tāmen jùjué le.
My wife offered to leave them a bunch of keys, but they refused.
段落 ③ · 第 6 句
那天，父亲喝醉了，等他醒时，天色已晚。
Nà tiān, fùqīn hē zuì le, děng tā xǐng shí, tiānsè yǐ wǎn.
That day Father got drunk; by the time he woke, it was already late.
段落 ③ · 第 7 句
我和妻子强烈要求父母在新房住一夜，第二天再回，但他们仍坚持坐上了最后一趟回老家的车。
Wǒ hé qīzi qiángliè yāoqiú fùmǔ zài xīnfáng zhù yí yè, dì-èr tiān zài huí, dàn tāmen réng jiānchí zuò shàng le zuìhòu yí tàng huí lǎojiā de chē.
We pressed them hard to stay the night and go back the next day, but they still insisted on catching the last bus home.
段落 ④ · 第 8 句
一段时间后，我和妻子又准备去外地打工，新房只能上锁空着。
Yí duàn shíjiān hòu, wǒ hé qīzi yòu zhǔnbèi qù wàidì dǎgōng, xīnfáng zhǐ néng shàngsuǒ kōngzhe.
After a while my wife and I were getting ready to go away to work again, and the new flat could only be locked up and left empty.
段落 ④ · 第 9 句
临走那天，父亲从老家赶来送我们。
Lín zǒu nà tiān, fùqīn cóng lǎojiā gǎn lái sòng wǒmen.
On the day we were about to leave, Father came all the way from the old home to see us off.
段落 ④ · 第 10 句
父亲悄悄把我拉到一边说：“你妈说了，你还是留一串新房的钥匙给我们，要是我和你妈什么时候想来了，就住上几天，顺便给你们晒晒被子，打扫打扫卫生。”
Fùqīn qiāoqiāo bǎ wǒ lā dào yìbiān shuō: “Nǐ mā shuō le, nǐ háishi liú yí chuàn xīnfáng de yàoshi gěi wǒmen, yàoshi wǒ hé nǐ mā shénme shíhou xiǎng lái le, jiù zhù shàng jǐ tiān, shùnbiàn gěi nǐmen shài shai bèizi, dǎsǎo dǎsǎo wèishēng.”
Father quietly pulled me aside: “Your mum says you should leave us a bunch of keys to the new place after all. If your mum and I ever feel like coming, we can stay a few days — and air your quilts and give the place a clean while we’re at it.”
段落 ④ · 第 11 句
父亲说这话时，轻声细语，还红着脸，像个害羞的孩子。
Fùqīn shuō zhè huà shí, qīngshēng-xìyǔ, hái hóngzhe liǎn, xiàng ge hàixiū de háizi.
As he said it he spoke very softly, blushing, like a shy child.
段落 ⑤ · 第 12 句
转眼又是半年，我们回家时是一个深冬的夜里。
Zhuǎnyǎn yòu shì bàn nián, wǒmen huí jiā shí shì yí ge shēndōng de yèlǐ.
Half a year slipped by in no time; we came home on a deep-winter night.
段落 ⑤ · 第 13 句
下了长途车，儿子被冻得大哭。
Xià le chángtú chē, érzi bèi dòng de dà kū.
Getting off the long-distance coach, our son was so cold he burst into tears.
段落 ⑤ · 第 14 句
我和妻子想象着打开家门满是灰尘、冷冷清清的景象，觉得心里发寒。
Wǒ hé qīzi xiǎngxiàngzhe dǎkāi jiāmén mǎn shì huīchén, lěnglěng-qīngqīng de jǐngxiàng, juéde xīnli fā hán.
My wife and I pictured opening the door onto dust and emptiness, and felt a chill inside.
段落 ⑤ · 第 15 句
来到楼下，抬头一看，却发现自家亮着灯光。
Lái dào lóuxià, táitóu yí kàn, què fāxiàn zìjiā liàngzhe dēngguāng.
We got to the foot of the building, looked up — and found the lights on in our own flat.
段落 ⑥ · 第 16 句
上了楼，开门的竟是微笑着的父母，温暖的气息立刻扑面而来：暖气开着，水已温热，卧室床上的被子已铺好，厨房里飘来阵阵饭菜香……
Shàng le lóu, kāimén de jìng shì wēixiàozhe de fùmǔ, wēnnuǎn de qìxī lìkè pūmiàn ér lái: nuǎnqì kāizhe, shuǐ yǐ wēnrè, wòshì chuáng shàng de bèizi yǐ pū hǎo, chúfáng lǐ piāo lái zhènzhèn fàncài xiāng……
Upstairs, the door was opened by my smiling parents, and warmth rushed straight at us: the heating on, the water already warm, the quilt laid out on the bedroom bed, waves of cooking smells drifting from the kitchen…
段落 ⑦ · 第 17 句
父亲说：“你妈昨天接到电话，知道你们今天回来，今天忙了一天了。”
Fùqīn shuō: “Nǐ mā zuótiān jiē dào diànhuà, zhīdào nǐmen jīntiān huílái, jīntiān máng le yì tiān le.”
Father said: “Your mum got the call yesterday, knew you were coming today, and has been busy the whole day.”
段落 ⑦ · 第 18 句
原来父母要我留下一串钥匙，只是为了让我们回来时，能立刻感受到家的温暖！
Yuánlái fùmǔ yào wǒ liú xià yí chuàn yàoshi, zhǐshì wèile ràng wǒmen huílái shí, néng lìkè gǎnshòu dào jiā de wēnnuǎn!
So that was it — my parents wanted a bunch of keys only so that when we came home we would feel the warmth of home at once!
段落 ⑦ · 第 19 句
我鼻子一酸，流下了热泪……
Wǒ bízi yì suān, liú xià le rèlèi……
My nose stung, and hot tears ran down.

6 · 语法 — 以来 · 临 · 立刻 · 悄悄/偷偷

先看规则和例句，再做下面的小练习。

以来yǐlái

时间 + 以来
以来 marks a stretch of time that starts at some point in the past and runs up to now. It comes after the time expression, and the clause after it describes what has happened in that stretch.
改革开放以来，中国发生了巨大的变化。
Gǎigé kāifàng yǐlái, Zhōngguó fāshēng le jùdà de biànhuà.
Since reform and opening-up, China has undergone enormous change.
长年以来，父母很少离开老屋。
Chángnián yǐlái, fùmǔ hěn shǎo líkāi lǎowū.
For many years my parents rarely left the old house.
一直以来，“80后”这个词儿都含有年轻的味道。
Yìzhí yǐlái, “bā líng hòu” zhège cír dōu hányǒu niánqīng de wèidao.
All along, the word “post-80s” has carried a flavour of youth.
练一练 model answers
到中国以来，我学会了很多东西。Dào Zhōngguó yǐlái, wǒ xuéhuì le hěn duō dōngxi.
他们结婚以来，关系一直很好。Tāmen jiéhūn yǐlái, guānxì yìzhí hěn hǎo.
近几年以来，空气污染越来越严重。Jìn jǐ nián yǐlái, kōngqì wūrǎn yuèláiyuè yánzhòng.

临lín

① 临 + 地方　② 临 + 动作
临 has two jobs. ① facing / next to a place — 临街、临海. ② just before something happens — 临走、临睡前、临考试. In sense ② it is followed by a verb, never by a noun of time.
我想买一套不临街的房子。
Wǒ xiǎng mǎi yí tào bù lín jiē de fángzi.
I want to buy a flat that does not face the street.
临走那天，父亲从老家赶来送我们。
Lín zǒu nà tiān, fùqīn cóng lǎojiā gǎn lái sòng wǒmen.
On the day we were about to leave, Father came from the old home to see us off.

立刻lìkè

立刻 + 动词
立刻 = immediately, at once. It is an adverb, so it sits before the verb, never after it and never at the end of the sentence. 立刻 is a little more formal than 马上; 马上 can also mean “very soon” about the future, 立刻 stresses no gap at all.
温暖的气息立刻扑面而来。
Wēnnuǎn de qìxī lìkè pūmiàn ér lái.
A wave of warmth rushed straight into our faces.
我回来时，能立刻感受到家的温暖。
Wǒ huílái shí, néng lìkè gǎnshòu dào jiā de wēnnuǎn.
When I come home I can feel the warmth of home at once.
练一练 model answers
一下课，我立刻回家。Yí xiàkè, wǒ lìkè huí jiā.
我快被气死了！我立刻去找他。Wǒ kuài bèi qì sǐ le! Wǒ lìkè qù zhǎo tā.
A：你能帮我通知他这件事吗？　B：好的，我立刻通知他。Hǎo de, wǒ lìkè tōngzhī tā.

悄悄 / 偷偷qiāoqiāo / tōutōu

词语辨析
Both describe doing something without being noticed, but the focus differs.
悄悄 — the sound / the fuss is small: softly, quietly, without drawing attention. It may or may not be secret.
偷偷 — the concealment is the point: behind someone’s back, afraid of being found out; often a slightly guilty flavour.
Because 悄悄 is the wider word, 偷偷 can often replace it; 悄悄 cannot replace 偷偷 where the hiding is the whole point (偷偷看别人的手机).
父亲悄悄把我拉到一边说。
Fùqīn qiāoqiāo bǎ wǒ lā dào yìbiān shuō.
Father quietly pulled me aside and spoke — low-key, without making a scene.
她偷偷地把这件事告诉了我。
Tā tōutōu de bǎ zhè jiàn shì gàosu le wǒ.
She told me about it on the sly (nobody else was to know).

运用 — 背景 Background

人们常说，父母对子女的爱是无私的、伟大的。父母对孩子的关心和照顾，不只是在孩子小的时候。即使孩子已经长大、自己已经变老，父母们也会继续为子女付出很多，只不过，爱的方式和以前不同了。
Rénmen cháng shuō, fùmǔ duì zǐnǚ de ài shì wúsī de, wěidà de. Fùmǔ duì háizi de guānxīn hé zhàogù, bù zhǐ shì zài háizi xiǎo de shíhou. Jíshǐ háizi yǐjīng zhǎng dà, zìjǐ yǐjīng biàn lǎo, fùmǔmen yě huì jìxù wèi zǐnǚ fùchū hěn duō, zhǐbúguò, ài de fāngshì hé yǐqián bù tóng le.
People often say that parents’ love for their children is selfless and great. Their care does not stop when the children are small. Even when the children have grown up and the parents themselves have aged, parents go on giving a great deal — only the way they show that love has changed.

话题讨论 — speak

先自己说一说，再看参考答案。

你同意“父母对子女的爱是无私和伟大的”这种说法吗？
Nǐ tóngyì “fùmǔ duì zǐnǚ de ài shì wúsī hé wěidà de” zhè zhǒng shuōfa ma?
Do you agree that parents’ love for their children is selfless and great?
我基本同意这种说法，因为很多父母会在生活中关心、照顾和支持自己的孩子。
Wǒ jīběn tóngyì zhè zhǒng shuōfa, yīnwèi hěn duō fùmǔ huì zài shēnghuó zhōng guānxīn, zhàogù hé zhīchí zìjǐ de háizi.
你觉得课文中的这对父母一开始拒绝接受新房钥匙，原因是什么？
Nǐ juéde kèwén zhōng de zhè duì fùmǔ yì kāishǐ jùjué jiēshòu xīnfáng yàoshi, yuányīn shì shénme?
Why do you think the parents in the text refused the keys at first?
我觉得他们不想给孩子增加负担，也不想让孩子觉得他们需要依靠孩子。
Wǒ juéde tāmen bù xiǎng gěi háizi zēngjiā fùdān, yě bù xiǎng ràng háizi juéde tāmen xūyào yīkào háizi.

Qǐng shuōshuo nǐ jìde zuì qīngchu de fùmǔ guānxīn nǐ, ài nǐ de yí jiàn shì.
Tell us the clearest memory you have of your parents caring for you.
我生病的时候，父母一直陪着我，照顾我的生活。他们虽然很累，但是从来没有抱怨过。
Wǒ shēngbìng de shíhou, fùmǔ yìzhí péizhe wǒ, zhàogù wǒ de shēnghuó. Tāmen suīrán hěn lèi, dànshì cónglái méiyǒu bàoyuàn guo.

命题写作 —《最深的爱》

  • 开头：说明观点 —— 父母的爱是最深的爱。
  • 中间：用课文的例子 —— 钥匙、打扫、被子、饭菜。
  • 结尾：谈自己的感受 —— 爱常常藏在小小的行动里。
父母对子女的爱往往是最深的爱。课文中的父母一辈子住在农村老家，他们虽然不愿意接受儿子留下的钥匙，但是心里一直惦记着孩子。儿子和妻子外出打工以后，父母悄悄来到新房，打扫卫生、铺好被子，还准备了饭菜。孩子回家时，他们立刻让孩子感受到家的温暖。我觉得父母的爱不一定总是通过语言表达出来，有时候一个小小的行动就能让我们感受到他们的关心。
Parents’ love for their children is often the deepest love of all. The parents in this lesson spent their whole lives in the old village house. They would not take the keys their son offered, yet their children were never out of their thoughts. Once the couple had gone away to work, the parents quietly came to the new flat, cleaned it, made up the bed and cooked. When the children came home, they felt the warmth of home at once. I think a parent’s love is not always put into words — sometimes one small action is enough to let us feel how much they care.
