Family word list (warm-up + 扩展)
表 cousins = children of dad's sisters or of mum's brothers and sisters. Children of dad's brothers are 堂 cousins (堂哥, 堂妹).
以来 yǐlái — since (then until now)
Pattern: time / event + 以来
长年以来，父母很少离开老屋。
For many years (up to now), my parents rarely left the old house.
临 lín — two meanings
① facing, next to: 不临街的房子 a house not facing the street
② just before (临 + verb): 临走那天 the day just before we left
立刻 lìkè — immediately
Pattern: 立刻 + verb
温暖的气息立刻扑面而来。
立刻 vs 马上: 立刻 = right this second. 马上 can also mean “very soon”: 春节马上就要到了 (a week away) — 立刻 does not fit there.
